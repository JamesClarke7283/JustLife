Final prompt:

Create a finished widescreen 16:9 launch splash screen for the original life simulation game JustLife. This is a new composition, using the attached image only as the exact established brand icon reference. Preserve the icon's ivory house, coral heart, sprouting sage leaves and teal rounded tile design. Place that icon centered above the title, scaled modestly, about 24% of the canvas height. The word must be spelled exactly JustLife. Calm cream #f8f6ef background with an extremely subtle warm paper finish and soft painterly sage botanical silhouettes disappearing into the far lower-left and upper-right corners; keep the center clear and uncluttered. Center the combined icon and wordmark as one balanced lockup, lots of breathing room, polished and inviting. Gentle warm light and a delicate shadow beneath the icon, no hard frame. Widescreen landscape, 1920x1080 or equivalent 16:9. The title and icon entirely inside the central 60% safe area so the screen scales gracefully. The ONLY text anywhere is 'JustLife'. No tagline, no buttons, no loading bar, no other logos, no Sims imagery, no people, no watermark. Output just the finished full-bleed splash screen image, not a screen mockup or device photo.
