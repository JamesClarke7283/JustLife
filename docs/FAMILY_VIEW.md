# Family view

The creator's **Connections** panel now offers Housemates, Siblings, Partners, My parent and My child. Parent choices are relative to the selected Lifelet; the stored edge always points from parent to child. Shared parents establish siblings automatically. Derived grandparents and other kin appear in the panel without becoming editable synthetic parent links. Invalid changes retain the existing draft and explain the conflict.

In live play, open **My Lifelet → Family tree**, or **People → All relationships → Family tree**. Every household member has their own portrait, name and age. Select a name to read relationships relative to that Lifelet; this does not switch the controlled character. Teal lines connect parents to children, coral lines connect current partners, and muted lines show declared siblings. Parents with a shared child and compatible partner pairs align within a generation. An unusual partnership that contradicts ancestry keeps its own row instead of making the layout cyclic.

Three ordinary generations fit at 1440×900. Wider or deeper families use the diagram's scroll area. Opening the view pauses live play; closing it restores the prior speed, including an already paused state. The underlying graph and directional relationship labels persist with a named household save.

Current limits: the graph covers the eight living household members; no deceased or off-household relatives, pregnancy, birth/adoption flow, parenting actions or inherited appearance are represented yet. A partner is not automatically declared a child's parent. Affinity and step-family labels are not yet derived. Same-stage parent setup is unavailable until chronological ages exist, but already established parenthood survives birthdays into matching stages.
