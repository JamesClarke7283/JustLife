# LifeSim integration

`scripts/life_sim.gd` is a `Node` with class name `LifeSim`. It does not process itself. Instantiate it, add it to the scene tree, and call `sim.tick(delta)` from the world's `_process`. One real second advances one game minute at speed 1, so a full Normal-speed day lasts 24 minutes. Supported speeds are `0`, `1`, `3`, and `8`. Clock starts at day 1, 08:00. Need decay and action durations use game minutes.

## World and character setup

- `new_household(profile: Dictionary)` resets all household state. Profile fields `name`, `traits` (up to three of Creative, Outgoing, Active, Bookworm, Foodie, Neat), and `aspiration` (Maker, Connected, Successful, Balanced) drive simulation. Other appearance fields are preserved unchanged.
- `register_targets(targets: Array)` supplies autonomy with `{id: String, kind: String, position: Vector3}` entries. Re-register when furniture moves or household presence changes. Autonomy considers actions after 15 idle game minutes, scheduling school/work/homework as well as recovery for needs below 52.
- Set `autonomy` directly; call `set_speed(value)` for clock control.

## Interactions and movement

1. `get_actions_for(kind, target_id = "")` returns an Array of dictionaries with `id`, `label`, `duration`, `changes` (need deltas), `cost`, `skill`, `xp`, `description`, `available`, and `unavailable_reason`. Supplying the target makes social requirements accurate for that Lifelet. Supported kinds: fridge, stove, sink, dining, counter, coffee_table, rubbish_bin, bed, shower, toilet, sofa, bench, tv, guitar, violin, bookshelf, easel, desk, plant, neighbor. A bench offers relax, read and nap. Sinks offer wash_hands and brush_teeth; tables offer clear_table; a full bin offers empty_bin; a fridge offers put_in_fridge for food left out. The easel, desk, bookshelf, treadmill, stove and lot exit also carry the emotion- and trait-gated interactions listed under *Emotion- and trait-gated interactions*. Aliases kitchen, bath, chair, computer, maya, leo also work. Recipe ingredients cost ℒ12 (garden skillet), ℒ16 (herb pasta) and…
2. `queue_action(id, target_id = "", target_position = Vector3.ZERO) -> bool` queues one of at most eight actions. IDs include snack, cook, sleep, nap, shower, toilet, wash_hands, brush_teeth, relax, watch, watch_together, read, study, study_book, buy_book, practice_instrument, paint, work, study, job, school, school_day, career_day, homework, help_homework, water, empty_bin, clear_table, put_in_fridge, friendly, joke, deep_talk, flirt, argue, ask_partner, commit, break_up. School/work departures use a registered `lot_exit` target; cooperative homework is coordinated by `LifeHousehold`. Finishing a toilet visit queues `wash_hands` at a reachable sink on its own, and a school or work day meets hunger, bladder and hygiene while away.
3. `action_started(action)` emits when an action becomes first in the queue, with `phase = "approach"`. Move the character to `target_position`, then call `begin_current_action()`. Calling it repeatedly is safe. Money is charged at this point, only once.
4. The action becomes `phase = "active"`; `elapsed` advances in game minutes, `progress` ranges from 0 to 1. Need and skill changes accrue during activity. The world can animate based on its `id`.
5. `action_finished(action)` emits on completion and the next queued action starts. Completion bonuses (income, relationship changes, wants) happen once.

`get_current_action()` returns the front dictionary or `{}`. Public `action_queue` exposes the whole queue; later entries have phase `queued`. `cancel_action(index = 0)` removes a queued or active item and advances the queue when needed. Started activities do not refund ingredients; partial need and skill gains remain. Social target IDs may be `maya`, `leo`, or a household member ID. The exact aliases `neighbor_maya` and `neighbor_leo` also resolve.

## Reconsidering blocked autonomy

`reconsider_waiting_autonomy(blocked_target_ids: Array, waited_game_minutes: float) -> bool` replaces only an **autonomous front action in approach**, after at least 30 game minutes of waiting. It excludes unavailable objects, considers recoverable needs below 52 in ascending order, and can choose another object or another depleted need. A blocked bed can therefore lead to a sofa nap; a blocked bed and sofa can lead to food or another available recovery activity. The returned `true` means the replacement was queued and its normal movement signal emitted. Later queued actions remain unchanged.

The waiting method preserves explicit player-directed actions, disabled autonomy, coordinated sessions, active actions and brief waits. The controller checks availability and retries an unsuccessful reconsideration after another 15 game minutes; LifeSim does not reserve furniture or move actors. The shared decision policy includes due school, homework and employment, forecasts physical needs before departure, and uses completed contact history to vary social choices. Scheduled responsibilities yield to urgent recovery; paid short recoveries are protected from repeated cancellation. Active autonomous actions can be reconsidered separately, while explicit later instructions and cooperative sessions keep their order and ownership. `autonomy_state` persists bounded contact history and deferred responsibilities. `tests/test_autonomy_policy.gd` and the school/career adversarial suites cover these rules; the original waiting suite covers alternative recovery and directed/paired preservation.

## Relationships and adult partnerships

`get_action_availability(action_id, target_id = "") -> {available, reason}` exposes the same gate used by the menu and action queue. Romantic eligibility is checked when queued, again on arrival, and again when a partnership action finishes. An unknown target is rejected instead of silently changing a different relationship.

| Action | Requirements | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ask_partner` | Both adults; both available; at least 45 friendship and 35 romance | Establishes a partnership |
| `commit` | Current partners; both adults; at least 65 friendship and 65 romance | Records a shared commitment |
| `break_up` | Current adult partners | Ends the partnership; friendship −12 and romance −35 |

Household pairs must meet the friendship and romance thresholds on both sides. `LifeHousehold` supplies actual partner, adulthood and reciprocal-score context, synchronizes accepted changes to the receiving Lifelet, and prevents multiple members from partnering the same neighbor. A couple's partner IDs and relationship stages must be reciprocal in saved data. Relationship milestones do not grant money or satisfaction.

`character.life_stage` and each relationship's `life_stage` use `adult`, `minor` or `unknown`. The existing adult-only avatar saves migrate to explicit `adult` values. Only `adult` participants can use romantic interactions; the other values are data guards, not child or aging features. New relationships also have `bond` (`none`, `partners`, `committed`, `separated`) and a `milestones` array. `romantic_partner` holds the current partner ID or an empty string.

`social_history` retains the latest sixty `{day, minutes, target_id, target_name, stage, label, detail}` entries, newest first. Stages are meeting, friendship, close friendship, a romantic spark, partnership, commitment and separation. Friendship milestones record once; a new partnership after a breakup is a new event. Both playable participants receive their own history. These memories also appear in the Lifelet's general diary. The actor uses the existing original wordless voices and conversational gestures for the three new interactions.

## UI state and signals

- `changed()` emits every 0.2 simulation seconds and on user mutations; `notice(text)` emits player-facing events.
- `needs`: hunger, energy, hygiene, bladder, fun, social, each float 0–100.
- `funds`, `day`, `minutes`, `speed`, `satisfaction`, `bills_paid_total`, `bills_late`, `last_bill_day`: numeric values. `utilities_cut` is a bool; `pending_bill` is the outstanding bill record.
- `character`: full profile, including name, appearance fields, traits and aspiration.
- `skills`: cooking, creativity, charisma, logic, gardening, parenting, fitness, music; each `{level: int, xp: float}`. Next level requires `level * 50` XP; cap 10. A skill book (`LifeHouseholdFlow.BOOK_SKILLS`, one entry per name in `LifeSim.SKILL_NAMES`) teaches any subject up to **level 9** and no further; the computer's mastery actions (`computer_<skill>`, offered on the `computer` kind) are the only route to **level 10**, and are refused with a reason once a subject is mastered.
- `relationships`: maya and leo, each `{name, friendship, romance, status}`. Friendship ranges −100 to 100, romance 0–100.
- `career`: `{track, title, level, performance, salary, worked_day, schedule}`. `CAREER_TRACKS` offers studio, culinary, technology, community, fitness, botany, **criminal** (ℒ1000/day, entered freely — no skill, no fee — Charisma) and **technical** (ℒ900 course fee and Logic level 8 to enter; Logic). `career_entry_error(track_id) -> String` is the single entry gate: non-empty is the player-readable reason the track is refused, and it is what the career picker uses to disable an unavailable row with that reason as the tooltip. `choose_career(track_id) -> bool` applies the same gate, charges the track's entry fee once on success, and otherwise changes jobs unless a shift is queued or the member is away. `LifeCareerSchedule` validates attendance, missed weekdays, lateness and the last paid date. Ordinary work and home shifts share one daily payment marker; freelance work is unrestricted. Earned pay/performance can produce a promotion and bonus. Missed weekdays reduce performance. No automatic wages. Household bills are issued by the first Lifelet each `BILL_PERIOD_DAYS` (7) for `BILL_BASE + home_value/1000 * BILL_RATE_PER_1000_VALUE`, fall due `BILL_DUE_DAYS` (4) later, and are paid from the phone (`pay_bill() -> {ok, paid, restored_utilities}`). An unpaid bill past its due date adds `BILL_LATE_FEE` (ℒ60) once and cuts the utilities, which refuses every action in `UTILITY_ACTIONS` until the arrears are settled. `pending_bill` is `{amount, issued_day, due_day, late_fee}`; `bills_paid_total` and `bills_late` are the ledgers. `household_bills_enabled = false` disables the ledger for other Lifelets in the same household, and they instead carry a read-only mirror of the owner's bill so their action availability agrees with it. A **burglar** breaks in every `ROBBERY_PERIOD_DAYS` (3) nights on the shared clock, taking `ROBBERY_LOSS` (ℒ600, capped at the purse so funds never go negative) and saying so in a notice. **Home insurance** answers it: `LifeSim.insurance_policy() -> Dictionary` and `LifeHousehold.insurance()` return `{}` while uninsured, and `buy_insurance(policy_id = "home") -> {ok, error}` charges the `INSURANCE_POLICIES` premium once; a repeat purchase is refused with a reason, nothing is refunded on cancel, and while cover is in force a break-in is reimbursed in full in the same breath. The policy round-trips through `get_state()`/`restore_state()` as `insurance_policy_id`, is mirrored to every member, and an unknown id is refused by `_validate_state` and by `LifeHousehold.restore_state`.
- `wants`: `{id,label,description,progress,target,reward,complete}` dictionaries. Completing each pays its reward in satisfaction and household funds once.
- `get_mood()`: `{label, description, color: Color}`; `get_clock_text()` formats `Monday · Day 1 · 08:00 · Your workday` — the weekday, the day number and whether the day is a workday, school day, day off or a spirit's rest. `day_kind_label() -> String` returns just that last part, so a caller can show the kind of day without the clock.
- `get_state()` returns a deep snapshot suitable for inspection; it includes live Vector3 queue positions.
- `moodlets` contains up to eight timed `{label, emotion, description, remaining, strength}` effects. `memories` keeps the latest forty `{day, minutes, label, detail}` entries. `add_moodlet(...)` and `remember(...)` add entries; moodlets expire with game time and influence `get_mood()` after critical needs are considered.

## Sustained aspiration progression

The opening chapter has three wants. Children begin with **A little independence**, fulfilled by a completed fridge snack, because their stove cooking is unavailable. Other ages begin with the first cooked meal. Old uncompleted child cooking wants migrate to the attainable snack goal without paying a reward; already fulfilled wants remain intact. After all three complete, the chapter is archived and the next chapter opens the following morning. There are always three current wants. Completed wants remain visible until the next chapter opens, and each reward pays only once.

`get_aspiration_progress() -> Dictionary` returns `{stage: int, title: String, next_stage_day: int, history: Array}`. A zero `next_stage_day` means the current chapter is still in progress. `aspiration_history` keeps the latest sixty completed chapters, newest first, with `{stage, title, completed_day, reward, wants}`. Historical rewards are accounting entries and are not paid again on loading.

Recurring Maker chapters combine paintings, other creative interests and friendly conversations on two days. Connected chapters combine Charisma practice, three conversation types and friendships maintained across two days. Successful chapters combine new earned income, practice in the career skill and varied recovery activities. Balanced chapters combine two days with every need at least 55, varied activities and company. Targets grow moderately through chapter seven and then stay bounded. Practice still counts at skill level ten, so late chapters remain achievable.

Additional fields on recurring wants are `metric`, `actions`, `skill`, `seen` and `seen_days`. The simulator owns these counters. Partial practice accrues with activities, while action counts and variety require completion. Income goals count completed work and painting payouts; story grants and want rewards do not satisfy those income goals. Social-day and self-care-day goals count each calendar day only once, including after a JSON save/load.

## Spending satisfaction: the Rewards store

Satisfaction from completed wants and story choices is spendable. `REWARDS` declares eight entries, each `{label, description, cost, permanent, effect}`. `available_rewards() -> Array` returns each as `{id, label, description, cost, permanent, owned, affordable}`. `can_buy_reward(id, reason = null) -> Dictionary` mirrors `get_action_availability`, returning `{available, reason}` for an unknown, unaffordable or already-owned reward. `buy_reward(id) -> bool` deducts the exact cost, applies the effect and emits a notice; a refusal changes nothing.

Five rewards are permanent perks recorded in the saved `purchased_perks: Array[String]`: Steel Bladder (bladder decay ×0.7), Hardy Constitution (energy ×0.85), Carefree (fun ×0.85), Great Kisser (flirt romance ×1.35) and Connections (every career performance gain ×1.25). Their decay multipliers are read at the same call sites as the trait multipliers, and the performance multiplier wraps every performance gain (freelance work, home shift, career day and story projects). Three are one-use potions that act immediately and are not recorded: Instant Meal (hunger to 100), Rejuvenating Soak (energy and hygiene to 100) and Inspiring Presence (grants the Inspired mood for 480 minutes). `get_state()` carries `purchased_perks`; the restore path reads it and the validator rejects any unknown id, non-permanent reward or duplicate, so a corrupted list fails the whole save.

## Emotion- and trait-gated interactions

Five interactions are offered only while the Lifelet's `get_mood()` label is the named emotion, and six only to a Lifelet carrying the named trait. The gate lives in `get_action_availability()` before every target rule, so a caller that never sees the option and one that queues it directly are refused with the same reason: **Paint a masterpiece** (Inspired, easel; sells for ℒ130 + 60/creativity level, ×1.5 while Inspired), **Study hard** (Focused, desk/computer), **Push through** (Energized, treadmill), **Playful prank** (Playful, a social action whose big friendship swing backfires into the Tense moodlet below 30 friendship) and **Bold introduction** (Confident, a larger friendship gain than an ordinary chat). Each carries its own `_activity_emotion`, so its skill gain still benefits from the mood that unlocked it.

Trait habits, one per trait: **Sketch for fun** (Creative, easel), **Host a chat** (Outgoing, sofa/loveseat/armchair/bench, which also raises nearby housemates' social need), **Morning run** (Active, lot exit; an Active adult sees a HUD button beside Go to work), **Deep read** (Bookworm, bookshelf/book nook), **Experiment with a recipe** (Foodie, stove; fun and creativity) and **Deep clean** (Neat, sink or surface; hygiene-adjacent with a small fun gain). A queued emotion action is re-checked on arrival, so a mood that has passed cannot still pay for the activity. All additions register through `_define(...)` in `_build_actions()` and appear in the ordinary menus.

## Daily story choices

Call `get_story_events() -> Array` to show pending choices. Each event is `{id, kind, day, title, description, choices}`. Each choice is `{id, label, effects: String, available: bool, unavailable_reason: String}`. `effects` lists the exact costs, payouts, need deltas, XP, friendship and career changes. Availability is recalculated from the current funds and skills whenever the API is read.

`choose_story_event(event_id: String, choice_id: String) -> bool` applies the selected effects immediately, records the decision, emits `changed`, and removes that event. It returns false without changing state for unknown IDs or unmet requirements. The event is removed before effect notifications, preventing reentrant listeners from paying it twice. Choosing a story does not advance time, move the Lifelet or replace the action queue. Need and relationship changes respect their normal bounds. Story choices can be made while the clock is paused.

One opportunity is offered each midnight starting on day two, cycling through neighbor supper, career project, hobby exhibition, garden exchange, library learning circle and community picnic. There are at most three pending opportunities. Unanswered events remain available without penalties; a full pending list skips new offers until space is available on a later day. The recurring cycle alternates neighbor context and captures relevant career/creative skill context at the time of the offer. Every event has an available option that requires no funds.

The first event is `story_day_2`, **An extra place at the table**:

| Choice ID | Label | Effects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bring_dish` | Bring a homemade dish | Pay ℒ24; Hunger +12, Social +24, Fun +10, Energy −8; Cooking +20 XP; Maya friendship +14 |
| `walk_together` | Suggest a walk together | Energy −10, Fun +15, Social +18; Gardening +12 XP; Maya friendship +8 |
| `keep_day_free` | Keep today to myself | No cost or stat changes |

`story_history` keeps the latest sixty decisions, newest first: `{event_id, kind, title, offered_day, day, minutes, choice_id, choice_label, effects}`. Raw `story_events` contains compact internal tickets; use `get_story_events()` for display. Event identity/context, chapter progress and decision history are validated before a save is applied. Old saves without these fields load with chapter one and empty story histories.

When using `LifeHousehold`, apply a choice to the selected member's simulator and call `household.adopt_selected_changes()` to immediately synchronize its funds change to the shared wallet before refreshing household UI.

## Save / load

`save_game(world_data: Array = []) -> bool` writes a validated-format JSON snapshot atomically via a temporary file to `user://justlife_save.json`. Arbitrary world placement dictionaries are passed through; Vector3 values become numeric `[x,y,z]` arrays and Colors become HTML strings. The world owns decoding its own placement fields.

`load_game() -> Dictionary` returns `{ok: true, world: Array}` on success or `{ok: false, error: String}`. Corrupt or unsupported saves do not mutate live state. `restore_state(state)` uses the same validation for in-memory snapshots and is useful in tests. Queue actions resume with preserved elapsed time and ingredient payment, but first enter `approach` so the world can position the actor before `begin_current_action()`. Connect signals before loading, or inspect `get_current_action()` afterward.

Tests include `tests/test_simulation.gd`, `tests/test_progression.gd`, `tests/test_household.gd`, `tests/test_story.gd`, `tests/test_relationships.gd` and `tests/test_depth_v61.gd`. The story suite completes the initial Maker goals, plays a second chapter across days two and three, reaches chapter three on day four, makes three different choices, and performs a real JSON save/load. It also verifies pending limits, free declines, skill-gated choices, reentrancy protection, skill-cap progress and legacy/corrupt saves. Relationship tests cover both adult gates, mutual score requirements, unavailable/stale proposals, staged history, commitment, breakup, reciprocal household state, neighbor exclusivity and JSON persistence. Run these in an isolated test project without the editor MCP autoload.

## Schooling and birthday transactions

`school_day` and `career_day` target `lot_exit`. The controller must route to the actual sidewalk before calling `begin_current_action()`. School runs weekdays 08:00–15:00, with lateness after 09:00; ordinary work runs 09:00–17:00, with lateness after 10:00. Both allow departure until noon and recheck eligibility, urgent physical needs and player instructions on arrival. These are original JustLife schedule choices. Departures do not reserve furniture.

`is_away()` covers both absent and returning phases. `get_away_state()` returns a defensive copy; `away_changed(state)` reports transitions. Version 1 records activity, phase, departure/return dates and minutes, actual exit ID/position, departure age, completion and `ended_at`; work also captures career track and salary. The end bell commits attendance, learning and proportional work pay once before return notifications. `request_return_home()` starts an early return without attendance/pay, preserving already-earned results after the bell. Only the controller's physical arrival callback, `complete_away_return()`, clears absence and begins later plans. Save/load preserves an absence or partial return walk; these actions retain their active state instead of restarting ordinary approach. Away bodies and pick collisions are hidden, unavailable members leave social targets, and household selection remains usable.

Each LifeSim stores `education`, validated by `LifeEducation`. Children and teens can queue `school` at registered desks/computers (label: **Attend online classes**, 180 minutes, weekdays 08:00–14:00) and `homework` at desks/computers/bookshelves (45 minutes, weekdays by 23:00). Completion applies learning and need effects once; cancellation grants no attendance or learning. Duplicate daily reservations, missing/wrong furniture, late arrivals, expired resumed classes, and incompatible age stages are rejected or cancelled. Saved school actions include the original furniture kind and start day/minute; strict validation checks duration, progress, dates, and eligibility.

Online classes remain an explicit alternative sharing attendance with `school_day`. The six-hour `job` home shift similarly shares paid attendance with `career_day`. New employment or enrollment after noon begins its attendance obligations the next day. Legacy home saves default to no absence and a neutral current-day career schedule. Active campuses/workplaces, per-career schedules and away activity choices remain unimplemented.

Daily updates finalize attendance. Birthday transitions archive the completed school stage, apply any earned graduation effects once, and clear incompatible schoolwork. Late-day enrollment starts attendance obligations on the following school day. Older saves without education receive a fresh term appropriate to their current age and calendar.

Birthday queue entries bind `birthday_from_stage`, so a natural birthday cannot cause a queued celebration to advance a second stage accidentally. Age, lifecycle history, education records, and queue cleanup commit before age/change/action-start observers run. An optional internal `celebrate_birthday(start_next_action=true)` argument lets the action-completion path start the surviving next action exactly once.

## Fresh familiar-face wish

New Lifelets receive a `familiar_chat` goal for “A familiar face”: complete a friendly chat, joke or heartfelt talk with a target whose friendship reaches at least35. Pre-existing family friendship alone does not complete it. Both participating household Lifelets can satisfy their own goal; rewards enter each participant's ordinary simulation tick so the shared wallet counts each one exactly once. Canceled partial actions do not count.

Older saved `friend` wants keep their absolute-friendship semantics, and completed rewards remain completed. The new goal uses existing action-progress fields and a one-conversation target. `tests/test_familiar_chat.gd` passes24 checks for fresh family start, threshold, cancellation, reciprocal participation, once-only rewards and old/new JSON persistence. The related simulation/story/progression/child-want/family/save and genealogy regression suites also pass.

## Shared meals

`LifeHousehold.meals` owns the `LifeMeals` ledger; `LifeMealFlow` binds it to world objects and character movement. The controller sets each simulator's `meal_service`. Cooking charges ℒ25 on arrival and creates four garden-skillet servings on completion, followed by `serve_meal`. Cooking itself grants no hunger. `eat_meal`, `store_meal`, `clean_plate`, `discard_meal` and `bin_meal` use the ordinary queue and real pickup/destination stages. `call_to_meal(batch_id)` invites available hungry household members without replacing existing plans. Fridge **Choose leftovers…** opens a picker rather than directly queuing an action.

Batches retain recipe, quality, initial/remaining/served/discarded counts, freshness, venue, host and carrier. Individual plates retain source batch, owner, seat, support offset, consumed fraction and shared company. A physical pickup claims one serving once. Eating delivers the remaining recipe nutrition over a 32-minute activity, preserves partial progress on cancellation and leaves a dirty plate when finished. Only the selected washed plate is removed. Fresh prepared food keeps for one game day at room temperature; refrigeration multiplies remaining freshness by 12, and taking a portion reverses that multiplier. Refrigeration does not reset age. Spoiled food cannot feed a Lifelet.

Spoiled food left out can be cleared two ways: **Clear this meal** carries it to the sink, and **Throw it in the bin** carries it to the rubbish bin, which takes one unit of rubbish as the food is tipped out. A spoiled dish on a table or counter shows a green mist, so a dish that has gone off reads at a glance rather than only being refused in its menu.

Transient `meal` and `plate` nodes render persistent food; they are excluded from furnishing serialization, navigation obstacles and Build sale/move commands. Placed dishes follow their host's transform. Pending furniture movement preserves food state until placement or cancel, while moving a diner's chair causes them to carry the same unfinished plate to their new place. Fresh active eating receives a short uninterrupted autonomous recovery turn; stale or expired portions do not gain that protection. Player instructions still retain ownership.

Saved food is validated with household queues before restoration: serving conservation, exclusive ownership, partial progress, action identity, freshness and company must agree. Full public saves also check the host transform, support height and the entire plate/platter footprint, including rotated furniture. Legacy saves without a meal ledger load with no prepared food. Only garden skillet is currently playable; the other recipe definitions reserve future content and do not establish ingredient or recipe breadth.

`tests/test_meal_state.gd` and `test_meal_autonomy.gd` exercise ledger and policy invariants with controlled arrivals. `python tests/run_playthrough.py --suite meals_build` performs actual cooking, dining, mouse-driven furniture movement, keyboard cancellation, named restart, leftover selection and washing. `test_meal_props.gd` verifies imported Blender geometry; `test_meal_gesture_render.gd` is a controlled directional art/contact study rather than a playthrough. See `REVIEWS/iteration_12_meals.md` for acceptance and remaining limits.


### Combined progression saves

Snapshots retain the weekly bill ledger alongside `whims`, wardrobe appearance, and the three passing-pressure timers (`starvation_minutes`, `exhaustion_minutes`, `deferred_passing_minutes`). Malformed supplied records are rejected before live state changes; older saves without these optional fields receive their defaults, and legacy `bills_paid` totals migrate to `bills_paid_total`. A spirit continues advancing the shared clock and household bill cycle, but no longer ages or celebrates birthdays. All memorial kinds offer remembrance, mourning, and flowers; mourning relief applies to both historical and current grief moodlets.
