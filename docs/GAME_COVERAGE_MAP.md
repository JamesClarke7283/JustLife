# Reference coverage map

Evidence milestone: 7 September 2026. Archive root: /home/impulse/Downloads/The Sims 4 References.

“Mapped” means a system's role in the flow is understood and representative screenshots were inspected. It does not mean every branch was captured, the feature is implemented in JustLife, or the source reflects every current patch. This is a research map, not a game completion checklist.

## Connected flow

New game/load → create household (identity, appearance, personality, aspiration) → select world → select home (price, furnishings, budget) → Live mode.

Live mode connects object/social actions to needs, emotions, skills, relationships, money and longer career/family progression. It switches to Build/Buy and back, opens phone/travel/events, and saves/resumes persistent state. World management supports moving/splitting/merging/rotating households and unplayed-household settings. Library/Gallery stores rooms, lots and households.

## Source and version discipline

[Interface In Game](https://interfaceingame.com/games/the-sims-4/) supplies readable full-screen layouts. The [EA screenshot article](https://www.ea.com/games/the-sims/the-sims-4/news/screenshot-tips-sims) supplies official activity/art samples. Carl's provides many annotated build/activity sequences but includes historical UI and mixed packs. Sims Community supplements infants, wants/fears and phone flows. EA Help supplies contemporary identity information. Current-rule implementation needs current primary-source verification.

Image filenames aid retrieval, not instructions. Mixed base/DLC evidence stays labelled. Modern captures can contain custom content or altered lighting: infant-care filenames visibly name a ReShade preset. Use those for pose/interaction study, not as an unqualified renderer baseline. Calendar capture source: [EA forum comparison thread](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/the-sims-4-general-discussion-en/calendar-comparison-seasons-vs-base-game/1210826), leading to the observed Postimages original.

## Creator follow-up, 8 September 2026

The browser exported 12 screenshots from [Carl's creator guide](https://www.carls-sims-4-guide.com/tutorials/how-to/make-a-sim.php) into `topics/creator_body_outfits_guide`. All 12 were inspected and all 12 have new content hashes relative to the existing archive at acquisition. `source_manifest.json` records original URLs, hashes, dimensions, duplicate comparisons and the browser export. The images range from 720×318 to 720×533. These are historical UI examples, with one annotated overview, rather than proof of every current rule.

The sequence shows new game, creator overview, gender settings, body sliders, face presets/detail-mode entry, clothing filters, swatches, accessory removal, multiple outfits, aspirations, traits and the Save Household affordance. It does not show genetics manipulation, actual drag deformation, the household-save dialog, or a complete move-in sequence. The broad-body shirt example has a shaped shoulder and turned sleeve cuff, with its hem falling over the belly; the front Henley example keeps long trouser shapes and restrained compression around the knees. Those visible garment principles inform original Blender work. No reference pixels or meshes are game assets.

A search result from G2A was rejected as a visual source because its header explicitly credited an AI image and it supplied no useful gameplay screenshots. No files were acquired from that page.

The same browser research exported one additional new 720×372 screenshot from [Carl's rewards guide](https://www.carls-sims-4-guide.com/rewards/) into `topics/reward_store_guide`. Its full dialog shows the selected character, available points, an open All/Traits/Potions filter, cost sorting, item rows and individual purchase buttons over a dimmed Live scene. The archive manifest pins its source and SHA-256 and records no prior duplicate. This fills the historical dialog-layout gap; it does not verify purchases, their effects, today's catalog or the expanded game's complete reward system. The page mixes core and expansion rewards, so its full written catalog is not treated as base-game-only evidence.

## Genetics selector follow-up, 9 September 2026

The browser exported and I inspected one new 1920×1080 screenshot from [Sims Community's December 2020 genetics bug report](https://simscommunity.info/2020/12/09/the-sims-4-skin-tone-genetics-issue-is-being-looked-into/), saved in `topics/genetics_selector_2020`. The original `.png` URL serves WebP; the archive preserves those bytes as `genetics-selector.webp`. Its source manifest records the browser export, dimensions and SHA-256 `946bf6738ef8c1430ee03eac7dcd2e370a9fdbc344591dc96cee743ccb81b52b`, with no prior duplicate.

The visible panel groups two parent portraits, a Child relationship dropdown, gender and age choices, Randomize Daughter, and cancel/confirm beside the full character preview. Human selection is active; dog/cat selectors are also present, so the capture is not a base-only installation. It establishes this historical panel layout, not the unpictured dropdown choices or the randomize-to-confirm sequence. The article documents faulty skin-tone inheritance: the displayed result must not be treated as intended inheritance behavior or evidence of the current game's rules.
