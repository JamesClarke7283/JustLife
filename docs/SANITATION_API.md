# Sanitation

`LifeSim` exposes `plant_wee` only when its bladder need is at most 12. It takes ten game minutes and gives +85 bladder and −12 hygiene continuously after reaching a real plant. The initial threshold is rechecked at arrival. A previously begun, paid action with retained progress may resume after saving or target movement even after partial relief passes 12. Removed or changed targets are reconciled through the normal action controller. Emergency pot use and mopping require empty hands; existing food carrying remains under the meal controller.

An empty bladder accumulates `bladder_grace`, in game minutes, after that step’s continuous effects. The exact fraction of a step spent at zero is counted. Relief resets the counter. At ten minutes, a visible on-lot actor produces one accident, restores bladder to 75, loses 35 hygiene and receives a 180-minute embarrassed moodlet. Resetting bladder prevents repeated events at the same zero crossing. Existing actions and later instructions remain intact. Pause, Build mode and paused menus do not advance the clock. School and career away states include bathroom breaks. Car travel, loading and invisible actors retain pending urgency until a real actor is visible in Live mode.

`LifeHousehold.sanitation` owns version-1 records with a monotonic serial and puddles containing `id`, household `member`, `venue`, `position`, `floor_y`, `level` and `created` absolute game minute. Validation checks typed finite values, member/place identities, unique serial IDs, floor consistency and cleanup ownership before replacing the household. Older saves default to no puddles and zero grace. Runtime nodes are excluded from furniture serialization, purchase/sale and navigation obstacles. A same-process world rebuild recreates puddles synchronously even when old scene nodes are still queued for deletion.

`mop_puddle` takes eight minutes. The existing physical approach and shared-resource controller arbitrate cleaners. Completion removes only that puddle; cancellation leaves it and removes the prop even while paused. When another cleaner finishes first, obsolete cleanup instructions are canceled while later actions survive. The original Blender mop is shown with both hands on the handle. Puddles are displayed above the measured ground or authored rug surface while retaining the navigation floor datum in saved state. This keeps outdoor wet patches on the lower sidewalk instead of floating at indoor height.

The current published runtime has one floor. Records carry floor/level information, but the separate staged two-storey runtime still needs integration with its physical-support provider and floor cutaway masks; stair-tread accidents are not yet qualified. Moving puddles by moving furniture, sanitation skill progression, environmental odors and autonomous mopping are outside this feature.

Run `TMPDIR="$PWD/dist/test-work" python3 tests/run_sanitation.py` on Linux, or add `--capture` for Forward+ scene captures. The runner copies scripts/scenes/assets into a private source, removes MCP configuration, sets both `XDG_DATA_HOME` and `JUSTLIFE_DATA_DIR`, restores an actual named save in a separate process, and checks actual car travel and independent messes across three homes. It never changes HOME. Test reports and screenshots stay in the private run folder.
