# Sanitation

`LifeSim` exposes `plant_wee` only when its bladder need is at most 12. It takes ten game minutes and gives +85 bladder and −12 hygiene continuously after reaching a real plant. The initial threshold is rechecked at arrival. A previously begun, paid action with retained progress may resume after saving or target movement even after partial relief passes 12. Removed or changed targets are reconciled through the normal action controller. Emergency pot use and mopping require empty hands; existing food carrying remains under the meal controller.

An empty bladder accumulates `bladder_grace`, in game minutes, after that step’s continuous effects. The exact fraction of a step spent at zero is counted. Relief resets the counter. At ten minutes, a visible on-lot actor produces one accident, restores bladder to 75, loses 35 hygiene and receives a 180-minute embarrassed moodlet. Resetting bladder prevents repeated events at the same zero crossing. Existing actions and later instructions remain intact. Pause, Build mode and paused menus do not advance the clock. School and career away states include bathroom breaks. Car travel, loading and invisible actors retain pending urgency until a real actor is visible in Live mode.

`LifeHousehold.sanitation` owns version-1 records with a monotonic serial and puddles containing `id`, household `member`, `venue`, `position`, `floor_y`, `level` and `created` absolute game minute. The additive optional `scale` defaults to 1 for older records. Validation checks typed finite values, member/place identities, unique serial IDs, floor consistency and cleanup ownership before replacing the household. Older saves default to no puddles and zero grace. Runtime nodes are excluded from furniture serialization, purchase/sale and navigation obstacles. A same-process world rebuild recreates puddles synchronously even when old scene nodes are still queued for deletion.

`mop_puddle` takes eight minutes. The physical approach and shared-resource controller arbitrate cleaners. Completion removes only that puddle; cancellation leaves it and removes the prop even while paused. When another cleaner finishes first, obsolete cleanup instructions are canceled while later actions survive. The original Blender mop's head follows the measured wet floor. Its hand IK runs after the body transform, including a zero-time paused-load reconstruction. The combined actor fits supported hips and both hands to the original shaft, including the actual floor-contact displacement. Its fourteen-body full-duration test passes unchanged hand and ankle limits, while the deep bend remains an acting limitation. Puddles are displayed above the measured floor or same-storey rug surface while retaining the navigation floor datum in saved state. This keeps outdoor wet patches on the lower sidewalk instead of floating at indoor height.

On both storeys, the complete patch footprint must have physical support and avoid walls and stair openings. Narrow standing points shrink the patch to a supported size while retaining the actor's exact position; an unsupported point keeps urgency pending. Owned stair entry, transit and clearance defer the event until actual supported arrival, preserving route identity, FIFO tickets and carried-food custody. A Lifelet on a floor hidden by the camera can still have an accident there. Wet-patch meshes and picking masks follow their own storey, so stacked positions cannot select the hidden patch. Build transactions and Undo validate existing patch support before changing physical geometry.

Physical snapshots read detached sanitation data without advancing urgency or movement. V2 loads validate support in every saved venue layout before constructing a candidate. The candidate owns its own sanitation flow, targets and views; failed loads discard them without touching the live world. Successful adoption rebinds the new flow and restores active pot/mop presentation at zero simulation time. Current approach/active sanitation actions must name a real target of the right kind in the current venue, and their saved standing destination must still match that physical target. An active Lifelet must already occupy that destination. These checks use the normal controller's 0.05m movement tolerance and preserve valid progressed pot actions whose bladder is already above 12. Completion also checks the current target before removing a puddle, so a damaged off-lot cleanup cannot erase another venue's record. Exact comparisons use the decoded save boundary and the production full-precision JSON writer. Courtesy movement uses the walking planner's existing 0.78m clearance, preventing a stationary housemate from indefinitely blocking cleanup at their own puddle while retaining the 0.72m physical collision separation.

Car boarding and driving animations advance no simulation time. The arrival transition applies its single fifteen-minute charge while still in travel mode; urgency can cap then, but a puddle appears only at the visible destination's first Live tick. Home, Maya's home and Leo's home keep independent records. Moving puddles with furniture, sanitation skill progression, environmental odors and autonomous mopping are outside this feature. The directed two-storey fixture and two-member car gate do not claim an eight-member sanitation endurance run.

Run `TMPDIR="$PWD/dist/test-work" python3 tests/run_sanitation_levels.py` on Linux for the combined producer/fresh/carrying/car/floor gates. `--only v2` and `--only car` select bounded groups; `--capture` runs only the Forward+ floor picking and mop capture gate. The runner copies scripts/scenes/assets into a private source, removes MCP configuration, sets both `XDG_DATA_HOME` and `JUSTLIFE_DATA_DIR`, and restores actual named saves in separate processes. It never changes HOME. Test reports, source hashes and screenshots stay in the private run folder.
