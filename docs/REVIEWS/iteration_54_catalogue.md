## What works

- `scripts/life_sim.gd` defines bath, practice_speech, play_piano, play_chess, jog, stretch, dance, play_toys, change_outfit and warm_up with distinct effects and moodlets; `restore_state` back-fills any missing skill at level 1 before validation, so the probe's six-skill save restores cleanly.
- Probe outcomes: bath from hygiene 5 reaches ≥90 with "Soaked and serene"; a piano session grants Music XP and "Music in the air"; a jog lowers hygiene and energy and grants Fitness; speech practice grants Charisma; a shower stays first for hygiene with bath as the fallback; a bored child on a free day chooses toys; an Active adult jogs on a free day but still relaxes briefly before a work morning; a Bookworm without books plays chess; an adult without easel, reading or television reaches for the piano; child autonomy never resolves a jog target.
- `scripts/world.gd` seat anchors for armchair/loveseat/stool and the piano bench, chess stool, tub and treadmill belt are consistent with the generated geometry; the computer desk reuses the desk chair search and desk surface.
- The paused in-game frame shows the treadmill, stool, piano and tub placed in the cottage with coherent scale and the runner standing on the belt holding the rails.

## Verification limits and scope remaining

No hands-on play, no game window, no packaged build. The developer's piano and bathtub sessions have no images in the evidence folder; their effects are confirmed only by the probe. Seventeen of eighteen models were seen only in the Blender render (auto-contrast crops), not in the game renderer. The wall-decor probe uses a synthetic one-wall layout. Probe and prework checks are component checks with supplied arrival, not routing or endurance evidence. Small-window layout, physical input, audio, save picker previews with the new models, home visits carrying the new kinds and the export pipeline were not exercised.

**Decision: iterate.** The simulation wiring and legacy-save handling are sound and can stay; the catalogue milestone is not accepted until the wall-decor flow, the two solid models and the age-rule gap are repaired with the evidence above. This is not a full-target claim.

## Three highest-value next iterations

1. **Wardrobe and creation identity.** The wardrobe exposes the same three outfits per age; add real garment and hairstyle variety with per-outfit previews in the creator, verified across child/teen/adult/elder frames and seated/raised-arm deformation.
2. **Live-mode depth with the new catalogue.** Two-seat and partner activities (chess opponent, dancing together, television from the loveseat), object state that shows (water, fire, an open wardrobe), and traced multi-day autonomy proving the new candidates improve days rather than add hygiene loops.
3. **Build depth.** Wall-mounted placement with snapping and consistent validation, room-aware placement feedback, catalogue navigation that scales (paging, search, counts), and more architectural and outdoor choices, each verified through purchase, save, reload and travel.
