## What works

- Deadline rule (`life_sim.gd:_duty_deadline`, `_leisure_fits`, `_brief_leisure_fits`): `minutes + 5 + duration + 10 ≤ 600` for work and `≤ 540` for school. Probe: 09:05 → relax, shift queued at 09:45, attended 1, late 0.0, Fun 43.6 at 17:00; 09:06 → career_day; 09:05 with the sofa excluded (read and watch are 60 minutes) → career_day; 09:25 → career_day; 09:35 at Fun 12 → career_day, at Fun 8 → relax then a 15-minute late arrival; a non-Creative adult at 09:05 Fun 46 projects above 20 and leaves, so the trait's 1.2× decay is what earns the break; a Bookworm behaves the same.
- Fitted pre-duty list: 08:40 with `[watch, read, relax]` in the history and a yoga mat → stretch (30 min), shift at 09:10, late 0; without the mat → relax; the developer's own cases (08:00 canvas still offered, 08:40 canvas withheld, critical boredom still paints) pass in the 58-check prework suite.
- Reconsideration keeps a fitting pastime: a watch begun at 08:40 is kept at 09:05 with 40 minutes left (545 + 40 + 10 = 595) and replaced with 50 left (605). During a wait for a busy seat the same reconsideration replaces the queued relax once it no longer fits, so the load blind spot in `_brief_leisure_fits` ends in an on-time departure.
- History persistence: `_finish_front` copies `_leisure_history` into `autonomy_state.leisure`; a JSON round trip restores `[watch, read, relax]`; `_validate_autonomy_state` rejects four entries ("Save contains an invalid pastime history.") and a non-leisure id ("Save contains an unknown recent pastime.").
- Third watcher: `_resolve_activity_target` assigns a slot to the trial action for two-seaters; the rendered loveseat suite passes 40/0 with "The third watcher takes the free armchair instead of queueing at the full loveseat" and "seated, not waiting"; `01_three_watchers.png` shows two on the loveseat and the third walking to the armchair (the capture precedes the seating).
- Whole-room paint (`building_edits.gd:_joined_walls`): a drawn 2×2 room quotes §48 for four walls and §36 when one wall already carries the colour; a repeat is refused ("Those walls already have this colour."), an unknown scope is refused, level 1 walls are painted only from level 1; join tolerance is 0.2 m (a wall ending 0.125 or 0.2 m from a corner joins, 0.25 m does not). On the legacy cottage `tx.current()` converts nine legacy records, the north-wall room coat quotes six walls for §280, commits, leaves six sky walls in the world state, undoes in one step with the full refund, and `_convert_proposal` on the unquoted path now answers "Painting a wall needs a build quote." with the wall count unchanged.
- Run 8 audit (`evidence/catalogue_week_run8/`, 318 assertions, 5 failures): jog 3, toys 9, piano 2, bath 1, dance 4, stretch 3, speech 10; every adult 5 shifts and every pupil 5 school days with homework; late minutes 0.0 for all eight (the latest of 40 departures was Casey at 09:38); Fun minima Robin 11.7 (day 5 evening into day 6, asleep at Fun 19 then a snack), everyone else ≥ 19.4; no critical minutes for any need.
- Frame budget: run 8's p95 fell from 81.4 to 60.5 ms and p99 from 105.9 to 87.4 on the same fixture and speed as run 6; the max is unchanged at ~139 ms.
- Annex routing (critic probe on the starter cottage with both annex sizes): the navigation graph stays connected — all eight sofa→exit and return→fridge routes resolve, the return spots are unchanged, and the annex interior is reachable from the sofa — so the freeze is not a missing route.

## Required next changes

**P1 — a room built in the front garden freezes the household.** Run 7 (annex 3×3 at x 2.6–5.6, z 5.8–8.8 with a west doorway): 17 completions in seven days, 59 failures, every member's minimum for every need 0.0, mean frame 73.4 ms (p90 130). The audit does not show a lot-edge problem: Parker froze at 16:19 on day 1 approaching the easel with a 23-point route and `path_index 0`, Robin at 16:34 approaching the toybox (0/34), Taylor at 17:36 approaching homework (0/15, waiting), all indoors and before any adult had returned; the adults came home at 17:02–17:07 to the return spot and then stood; from day 2 no member holds an action at all. My probe shows the graph connected with either annex, but the eighth member's lot-exit cell moves from (2.75, 8.5) to (3.0, 8.25) — inside the annex footprint — because `lot_exit_position` takes the nearest free cell regardless of rooms, so the exit row does overlap the garden room. The bisect evidence (`bisect_A/B/C.log`) is three exit-0 lines from one-day runs that skip the weekly assertions; the folders with the audits were not kept, so "frozen" and "73 completions" cannot be checked. Player impact: an ordinary build action (a room in the garden) turns the household into eight statues until reload. Acceptance: a rendered one-day run with the annex (either size) on the final code completes at least sixty actions with no non-waiting approach stationary for thirty minutes and its audit retained under evidence; `lot_exit_position` and `lot_return_position` never resolve inside a room (test in `test_world`); the cause is named in the review (route not advanced, segment rejected, or replanning every frame — the 3× frame cost suggests the last) and covered by a headless case; only then does the two-bed contract week run.
