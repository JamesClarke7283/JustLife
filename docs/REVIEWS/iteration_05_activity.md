## Visual assessment

These actions now communicate cooking and eating much more clearly than the preceding empty-handed gesture. The ordinary kitchen view retains appropriate scale and appliance proximity. The closer contact views are explicit inspection cameras placed inside the kitchen; they should not be mistaken for new player camera controls. The normal rotated overview is also retained and shows how an exterior wall can obscure a side view.

The most visible remaining contact limitation is the hand grip. The spoon is attached to an open palm, and the bowl and snack lack convincing curled-finger support. A restrained grip shape would improve this without requiring new props. The sharply folded inside elbow remains apparent in the larger isolated actor study, though this pass does not establish an actual mesh separation.

The interaction sequence is still simplified: the fridge does not visibly open, the snack is eaten facing it, and cooking restores Hunger while stirring without a separate served meal/eating stage. Props improve presentation, but a complete prepare/serve/eat/clean-up loop and shareable household meals would add meaningful depth. No such system is assumed from the new bowl appearing.
