## Visible progress

The old protruding white capsule impression is substantially reduced: skin
now encloses the eyes more convincingly in both views. Brows and the Bob's
directional flow remain readable. The creator is calm, legible and gives the
face useful space; grouping controls into Shape, Eyes, Nose and Mouth works
visually, although these images do not establish interaction quality.

The six generated faces have meaningful proportion differences. Marin has a
wider mouth and broader lower face; Devon is longer and narrower; Avery has
fuller cheeks and a shorter lower face. Finley's shorter nose is readable.
These are geometry differences, not palette substitutions. However, Devon
and Wren remain close, as do the rounder faces in three-quarter, where the
shared eye opening, nose-tip family and lip contour dominate recognition.

## Three highest-impact remaining fixes

1. **Finish continuous facial planes and feature transitions.** The hero and
   all generated faces show angular diagonal cheek dents and small pinched
   marks below the chin; Marin's front and three-quarter make both especially
   clear. Their repeated placement reads as a surface defect, not intentional
   facial anatomy. The nose-to-cheek boundary and lip edges also remain abrupt,
   while the improved lids lack a deliberate upper-lid edge and corner detail.
   Diagnose the dents before attributing them to topology, normals or lighting.
   Acceptance: clean cheek/chin gradients and integrated nose, mouth and lid
   transitions in both angles across the same six faces, with controlled skin
   tone/roughness variation surviving normal portrait size.
2. **Broaden coherent facial identity beyond length and fullness.** Preserve
   the useful mouth-width and face-length changes, then vary eye opening/tilt,
   bridge-to-tip profile, jaw angle and lip contour as coordinated facial
   forms. Current eye spacing and brow changes do not overcome the shared
   template. Acceptance: each of six same-style faces is easy to recognize
   when shuffled at the normal creator zoom, in front and three-quarter;
   stronger numerical slider differences alone are insufficient.
3. **Bring short hair and garment construction up to the Bob's finish.** In
   these captures Buzz reads as a smooth thick cap with a raised, irregular
   front rim. The pale shirt has inflated shoulder masses and deep sleeve/body
   joins, with little seam, collar or fabric definition. Refine the short-hair
   scalp fit/hairline and directional surface; give the shirt intentional
   shoulder volume, connected sleeve transitions and restrained construction
   cues. Acceptance: both read immediately as the intended hair and garment
   at portrait scale. The Bob is stronger but its thick lower edge and broad
   shell masses still warrant the prior hair review's clump/taper refinement.

**Decision: retain the improvements and iterate.** The eye integration and
identity range improved, but facial surface defects and uneven asset finish
remain plainly visible. The reference is a finish standard, not a request to
copy its character, proportions, assets or interface.
