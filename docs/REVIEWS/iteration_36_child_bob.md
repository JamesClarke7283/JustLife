# Iteration 36 — child Bob grouping and taper

The child's Bob had six similar rounded vertical locks with deep repeated clefts and blunt tips. The original-art revision combines those side locks into shallow connected flow and turns the lower band inward toward the jaw/nape. The swept fringe remains exact. Independent review accepts the change as an incremental replacement after inspecting 16 new matched pairs (32 actual images), in addition to four previously reviewed neutral pairs. No new ear/scalp pinhole or closed raised patch is visible in the sampled extremes.

The rear remains smooth and heavy. Close lower-detail rear views have real dark angular hem pinches, strongest in standard and broad left-rear views. Their cause is not established; they are accepted residuals for this bounded improvement, not claimed absent at every Live angle. This change does not raise the overall game score or establish a 10/10 character design.
