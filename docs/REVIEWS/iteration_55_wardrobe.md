## What works

Shorts and leg skin carry the `Sit` morph like the trousers, so seated deformation has the same corrective.
- Probe on the new exports (`tests/critic_probe_55.gd`, isolated project, HEAD scripts): configuring hair 5 / outfit 4 / bottom 1 shows exactly `Hair_Buzz`, `Outfit_Hoodie`, `Bottom_Shorts` and its cuffs with trousers and trouser cuffs hidden, brows visible, waistband and pockets kept, and the leg-skin material recolored to the skin swatch; `set_bottom(0)` restores trousers; `set_outfit(3)` shows the tee; all six child hairstyles show exactly one hair group each with the matching outfit and bottom; teen and elder show Long, Hoodie and Shorts.
- Probe on the previous exports (`Hair_Buzz`, `Outfit_Hoodie`, `Bottom_Shorts` absent): hair falls back to Crop, outfit to Casual, trousers stay visible, and `profile` keeps hair 5 / outfit 4 / bottom 1 for a later export.
- `LifeSim`: adult treadmill offers only `jog`, adult toybox nothing, child toybox only `play_toys`, computer offers `play_games` for adults and `school/homework/study/play_games` for children while the plain desk does not; `queue_action` refuses child jog and adult toys and accepts a teen jog; `change_outfit` advances 0→1 and wraps 4→0 with a notice; a child may use the wardrobe; adoption candidates for all seven serials stay within hair 0–5, outfit 0–4, bottom 0–1 and use the new styles; queued `play_games` and `warm_up` restore.
