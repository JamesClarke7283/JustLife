## What works

- `restore_state` drops queued `jog`/`play_toys` that fail `get_action_availability` for the restored character: child+jog → `{"ok":true}` with an empty queue and "Policy Lifelet: a saved activity no longer suits this age and was removed."; adult+toys likewise; `[play_toys, jog, read]` on a child restores as `[play_toys, read]`; a teen's jog is kept.
- `wall_snap` returns the expected position and angle for a painting, clock and shelf on synthetic north (0°), south (180°), west (90°) and east (−90°) walls and on both faces of an interior wall (x=1.13 facing +x, x=0.87 facing −x); `wall_behind` is true at those spots, false 0.3 m off the wall and for the item turned into the wall; 270° behaves as −90°; the 1 m reach is exact (no snap at 1.00 m, snap at 0.95 m). Both starter paintings (lots 0 and 1) have a wall behind at their shipped spots, can be re-placed there and snap within 1 cm; the five neighbourhood layouts validate and the studio loads its three paintings.
- `LifeSim.emotion_effect` multiplies `_apply_continuous_effects`: reading with a Happy moodlet earns 27.5 Logic (25 × 1.1), Tense 20.0, a jog while Energized 52 Fitness (level 2 with 2 XP left); a Creative painter's first canvas sells at 125 and the second, finished while "In a creative flow" is active, at 156.
- `promotion_requirement` at level 1 is `{creativity 1, met}`; at level 2 with Creativity 1 and performance 130 `_check_promotion` keeps level 2, sets performance to 100 and emits "Performance is excellent. Reach Creativity level 2 to become a creative specialist." once; a second check the same day is silent; with Creativity 2 the next check promotes to level 3.
- Wardrobe: outfit 0 offers `wear_jacket, wear_cardigan, wear_tee, wear_hoodie`; `wear_tee` sets outfit 3 and drops from the menu; a child may choose the hoodie; a queued wear action restores; `change_outfit` still wraps 4 → 0.
- GLB decode: `character_lod.glb` now 105,064 vertices (was 115,931); `Skin_Leg_continuous` 3,086 with no morph targets (13,953 in the full model); `Bottom_Shorts` 8,640 with the Sit morph; `Outfit_Hoodie_Hood` 91/392. The actor hides `Skin_Leg*` under trousers and shows it with shorts (probe).

## Required next changes

**P1 — half the second collection is never used in autonomous play.** In both rendered weeks, with the stereo, yoga mat and mirror present (and the piano in run 2), there were 0 completions of play_piano, dance, stretch and practice_speech; practice_speech, warm_up and play_games are not autonomy candidates at all, and for non-Active adults `_autonomy_need_choice` tries paint first while `_autonomy_target_for` returns the busy easel with a load cost rather than passing, so seven Creative Lifelets queue at one easel (38 sampled approach/queued paint states; 7.8–11.2 waiting hours per member per week, the bed and toilet queues on top). Player impact: bought furnishings sit idle and the household stands around. Acceptance: the catalogue week asserts each purchased activity furnishing completes at least three times across the household and reports per-member waiting ≤ 4 h/week; a busy first candidate yields to a free later one (the uncommitted load-aware choice, committed and rerun); at least one non-Active adult dances, stretches or plays the piano in the audit.

**P1 — Fun collapses across a shift.** Run 2: Morgan Reed's Fun fell to 2.9 with 121.7 minutes below 10 (day 3: 14 at departure, 5 on return at 17:00, recovered only after two canvases and television); Casey Vale 8.7 (30.6 min), Avery Reed 6.7 (30.2 min); run 1 had none, and iteration 51 recorded 36–59-minute zero-Fun spells from the same mechanism. `_autonomous_choice` lets a Lifelet leave for a scheduled day with Fun ≥ 12 while a Creative adult decays 3/h for eight hours. Acceptance: the departure rule projects Fun at the end of the shift and takes the brief leisure first when the projection is under 20; no member below 10 Fun in either catalogue week; both weeks' minima reported against iteration 51.

**P1 — wall-mounted placement is wrong at ±90° and in corners.** `can_place` swaps `size` for odd multiples of 90° before computing the forward shift, so the floor/blocked test rectangle of a wall item on an east/west wall is pushed into the room by half its *width* (+0.04) instead of half its depth: painting 0.64 m, shelf 0.50 m, clock 0.27 m. Probe: a painting snapped to the east wall with a parallel interior wall 0.71 m in front is refused (its test rect lands at x 3.18–3.28 over the wall at 3.08–3.22) while the identical case on the north wall is accepted; on the upper floor the same displacement tests the wrong slab against stair openings. `wall_snap` also clamps along the wall's full length, so a clock dragged into the north-east corner snaps to x=3.845 (spanning into the east wall) and `can_place` then refuses it with "That space needs a little more room." Acceptance: the shift uses the unrotated depth; painting, shelf and clock are accepted on east/west walls with a parallel wall or opening 0.5 m in front exactly as on north/south; the corner clamp subtracts the perpendicular wall thickness so a snapped ghost is always placeable; the probe's wall section passes; purchase, save, reload and travel verified for one item at each of the four angles.

Emotion precedence: `get_mood` returns the strongest moodlet before the activity's own label, so a reader with a Happy moodlet earns 27.5 Logic against 31.25 for a plain reader (active read → Focused ×1.25); any need under 40 masks every emotion ("Unsettled", 19% of run-2 samples); the +25% sale needs a prior Inspired moodlet and the closest two canvases by one painter were 7.6 h apart, so no bonus sale occurred in 53 autonomous canvases across both weeks. Frame time: p50 33.5 ms / p95 90 ms for eight Lifelets at very fast, unchanged by the LOD work; profile and report where the frame goes. Visual: the long hair's back sheet through the hood roll (all ages); one face per family; seated-in-shorts and raised-arm-hoodie frames from the production camera; a mirror that reflects or at least reads as glass; the toybox pose; Skills panel at 960×600. `validate_home_layout` still accepts a floating clock on load (lenient by design; say so in the save docs).
