# Inviting a neighbor over

From **People**, choose **Invite over** beside a neighbor once any household member has at least 20 friendship with them. Invite at home in Live mode. One invited neighbor can visit at a time. The normal starter home is supported through its existing navigation view; inviting does not convert or rebuild the saved construction layout.

The neighbor walks from their current sidewalk position or a clear curb point. On arrival, the persistent guest card offers **Welcome in**. Fast simulation slows to normal speed so the player can respond. The guest card shows the remaining welcome time and, once indoors, the remaining stay. Welcome queues the ordinary friendly conversation for the selected household member. Earlier work remains queued, and the guest enters only when that identified conversation actually finishes successfully. An ordinary untagged social interaction does not admit the guest. The neighbor waits up to 120 game minutes for the welcome to start, then stays up to 360 game minutes after actually reaching the indoor gathering point. Arrival and entry routes have a 180-minute limit.

**Say goodbye** is available throughout the visit. A conversation that is already active finishes with its ordinary effects; later conversations with the departing guest are canceled while unrelated household instructions remain. The guest follows a real exit route and becomes absent only at the sidewalk. Build mode and household travel wait for that departure. If another Lifelet blocks the path, move that Lifelet in Live mode to make room.

Named saves preserve arrival, waiting, the exact queued/active Welcome, entry, the indoor stay and departure, including route progress and conversation ownership. Active visitor saves validate their resident envelope, lifecycle, route and body placement before the existing household is replaced. Older saves without a guest record remain supported. The visitor remains a resident with their own home, rather than joining the player's household. Guest dining and party events are separate future work.
