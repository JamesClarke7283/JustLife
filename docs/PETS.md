# Pets

Original household pets. A Lifelet household can adopt cats and dogs, shape them in a pet picker, and buy the accessories each species uses. All pet art is original geometry generated by `tools/create_pets.py`; no third-party models, textures or reference bitmaps are used.

## Buying a pet

**Phone → Juniper Pet Shop** (or **Adopt a cat or dog**) opens the shop. A cat costs §320 and a dog §480. The price is charged once, at confirmation; canceling or closing the shop leaves the household and its funds exactly as they were, the same contract the phone's adoption flow uses.

The shop reviews six candidates per visit (`LifePets.candidate(serial, choice)`), which always include both species and both sexes, so no visit hides a species behind a reroll. A review is reproducible: the same serial and choice always describe the same animal, so reopening the shop out of order never changes what is offered.

Confirmation walks the pet in from the street to its own clear indoor spot — the same kind of arrival walk an adopted child takes, from the lot exit into the home — and it is refused with a reason when that route is blocked, before any money changes hands. The spot search prefers clear floor beside the household's food and water bowl, then the nearest clear floor tile to a few room seeds, and requires a real non-solid ground tile that the household's own placement rules leave standing-clear, so a pet never settles inside furniture or on top of a Lifelet; several pets spread out rather than stacking. Like adoption, repeating a confirmation returns the original receipt instead of charging twice.

## The pet picker

The pet picker is the person picker applied to an animal. It offers:

* **Species** — cat or dog, each with its own price and its own authored model.
* **Sex** — female or male. This names the pet and picks the default name pool; it does not change the model.
* **Name** — free text, up to 24 characters.
* **Base coat and second colour** — two choices from one shared list of 18 natural coat tones, so any pairing is reachable.
* **Mixed gradient** — a 0..1 slider. At 0 the coat is the base colour; at 1 it blends fully into the second colour.
* **Coat length** — short, medium or long, which also changes the silhouette (long sits slightly fuller).
* **Markings** — plain, bicolour, tuxedo, tabby, points or mask, which bias the marking zones toward the second colour.
* **Surprise me** — rolls a fresh candidate while keeping the species and sex already chosen.

A live preview beside the picker redraws as features change.

## How a mixed coat is drawn

`assets/shaders/pet_coat.gdshader` replaces the two authored placeholder surfaces, `Fur` and `Fur_Mark`, on the loaded model. It blends `primary_color` into `secondary_color` along the pet's height: the coat sits at the full base colour along the back and blends toward the second colour low on the body, so natural countershading falls out of the same gradient — a dark base with a pale second colour reads as a dark back over a lighter belly and pale paws. `gradient_blend` sets how far that blend reaches, and the marking zones are held at or above `marking_bias` of the second colour so a bicolour or tuxedo pet keeps readable markings even when the gradient is light. One continuous gradient therefore covers a solid animal, a soft two-tone and a strongly mixed coat without any hard edge where two flat colours meet. If the shader cannot be loaded the coat still reads correctly, just flat, because the fallback assigns the two colours directly.

`gradient` is stored per pet, so the saved animal always rebuilds with the same coat.

## Accessories

Accessories are ordinary catalogue furnishings under the **Pets** category, bought and placed through the normal Build & buy path, so reach, support and doorway checks apply unchanged.

| Item | Price | Offered when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Food & water bowl | §60 | the household owns any pet |
| Climbing cat tree | §240 | the household owns a cat |
| Garden dog kennel | §320 | the household owns a dog |

The shop only offers what the household can use and refuses the rest with a reason (`LifePets.accessory_kind_error`).

## In the home

Each pet gets a `LifePetActor` body in the world: its authored model, its mixed coat, and a small idle where the head turns, the tail sways and the legs stay planted, becoming a diagonal-pair trot while it walks in from the street. A paused household freezes its pets too. Clicking a pet opens its card with its species, sex, coat description and a live preview.

The body is presentation. The household owns the saved record, the fee and the arrival policy, so a save always resumes the same animals in the same places — on load, `main.sync_pets()` rebuilds every body from `household.pets`.

## Save format

Pets ride the household save as an optional `pets` record beside `adoptions` and `pregnancy`:

```
"pets": {"version": 1, "next_serial": 2, "pets": [
  {"id":"pet_1","serial":1,"species":"cat","sex":"female","name":"Willow",
   "coat_color":"3a2b23","mark_color":"f2ece0","gradient":0.7,
   "coat_length":"medium","marking":"bicolor","day":1,"fee":320}
]}
```

An absent record means a household that owns no pets, so older saves load unchanged. `LifePets.validate` rejects a damaged record — a duplicate identity, an out-of-order serial, an unknown species, sex, coat length or marking, an impossible gradient, or a price that does not match the shop — rather than silently dropping it.

## Files

| Path | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `scripts/pets.gd` | `LifePets`: prices, candidate rolls, appearance normalisation, accessory policy and save validation. Pure static policy. |
| `scripts/pet_actor.gd` | `LifePetActor`: the world body, mixed coat and idle animation. |
| `scripts/pet_shop_flow.gd` | `LifePetShopFlow`: the phone shop, the picker and the arrival confirmation. |
| `assets/shaders/pet_coat.gdshader` | The mixed-coat gradient. |
| `assets/models/pet_cat.glb`, `pet_dog.glb`, `pet_bowl.glb`, `cat_tree.glb`, `kennel.glb` | The original exported models. |
| `tools/create_pets.py` | Regenerates all five models. |
| `tests/test_music_and_pets.gd` | 105 checks covering the music switch and the whole pet path. |

Both animals expose the same named parts — `Body`, `Head`, `Tail`, `Ear_L`, `Ear_R` and the four legs — with the `Head` and `Tail` origins at their pivots, plus the materials `Fur`, `Fur_Mark`, `Nose`, `Eyes`, `Paw_Pads` and `Collar`. `tools/create_pets.py` regenerates them with `blender -b --python tools/create_pets.py`.
