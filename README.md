# JustLife

An original Godot life simulation with Lifelet creation, a shared household, home design, neighborhood visits and stories that continue across days.

Open `project.godot` in Godot 4.7.2, allow the assets to import, then run the main scene. To create a Linux build, use `python tools/export_linux.py` with matching Godot export templates installed; then open `dist/JustLife/JustLife.x86_64`. Build outputs are local and are not committed. A separately downloaded build runs through `JustLife.x86_64` directly. The Linux build uses Vulkan/Forward+; the verified desktop has an NVIDIA RTX 5090. Other graphics hardware has not yet been qualified.

## Playing

Start with **New game**, create up to 8 Lifelets, choose their age, looks, faces, outfits, personality and aspirations, then find a home. Child, teen, young adult, adult and elder stages have matching character models; children need a teen or adult housemate. Connections lets a household begin as housemates, siblings, partners, parents and children. Parents must be adults in an older age stage during creation. **My Lifelet → Family tree** shows the household’s generations; choose a portrait card’s name to read connections from that person’s perspective. Click furniture or a person to choose an activity. Each Lifelet walks to their destination before beginning. The household shares money and time, while each person has their own needs, skills, relationships and queue. Select a household chip to take control of another person.

**Phone → Adopt a child** lets an adult at home review three school-age Lifelets and choose one or two adult guardians. Confirmation costs §1,000 and requires a free household place; canceling keeps the family and funds unchanged. The child walks home from the street, joins the family tree and becomes controllable immediately. Their first school day is due after the settling-in day. You can pause and save during arrival while other household members keep their existing activities.

Children and teens have a **School** panel. **Go to school** sends them to class on weekdays, 08:00–15:00; arrival after 09:00 counts as late, with departures allowed until noon. Homework prepares the next attended class; lateness and absences affect grades. Adults can **Go to work** on weekdays, 09:00–17:00, with lateness after 10:00 and pay proportional to time at work. Lifelets walk to the street, remain away during the day, and walk home afterward. You can control other household members while they are away, save and resume an absence, or bring someone home early without attendance/pay credit. School and career records offer online classes and home shifts as optional alternatives that share the same daily credit.

Autonomy plans school, work and homework around urgent needs while preserving queued player instructions. School records follow a Lifelet into later age stages. **My Lifelet → Celebrate a birthday** advances one stage after a confirmed §30 activity. **Esc → Life settings** changes lifespan or pauses automatic birthdays for the household. Elders currently stay in their final stage.

At a desk, **Do homework together…** lets a child or teen learn with a trusted household adult. Both walk into position, share progress, and gain friendship and learning when they finish. The helper builds Parenting skill. Canceling from either participant ends the shared activity; saving preserves unfinished work. The HUD identifies the partner, and skill bars show progress toward the next level. Short completion messages appear in readable cards beside their speakers; the selected Lifelet has priority when a scene is crowded.

**Cook a fresh meal** opens a cookbook: garden skillet makes four servings for §25, herb garden pasta unlocks at Cooking 2 and makes four for §32, and harvest vegetable bake unlocks at Cooking 4 and makes eight for §52. Each recipe lists its preparation time. Ingredients are charged once when cooking begins; the cook carries the finished dish to a table, and eating restores hunger. Click the served dish to **Call everyone to eat**, take a serving or put away leftovers. Each diner uses a separate plate and available chair. When seats are unavailable, Lifelets walk to reserved standing places that remain clear of other settled activities; building over a dining place reroutes the same partial serving. Fridge leftovers remain selectable until they spoil. Finished meals leave dirty plates that can be carried to a sink and washed. Idle Lifelets may wash dishes after tending their needs and responsibilities. Dishes retain clear support when meals end or furniture is sold; idle Lifelets step aside when crowding another person. Partial meals and their ownership survive saving, and furniture moves preserve the same food. Pasta and bake use original dishes, plates and preparation gestures. The bake now uses an opening oven door and sliding rack: the Lifelet seasons, loads, bakes and retrieves the dish before serving. Paused saves restore the dish and pose during transfer or inside the oven, preserving the one ingredient charge and later instructions.

**Explore** opens Juniper Gardens, The Reading Room and Common Ground Studio. Travel takes 15 in-game minutes and brings the household together. **Stories** presents opportunities and personal memories as the days pass. Adult relationships can become partnerships and commitments once mutual friendship and romance requirements are met; breakups have lasting relationship effects. **Build & buy** at home places, rotates, moves and sells furnishings, builds rooms/walls, opens doorways and changes floors.

- Right-drag or Q/E: orbit. Mouse wheel: zoom. WASD/arrows: pan.
- Space: pause. 1/2/3: normal/fast/very fast time.
- B: Build & buy. R: rotate a furnishing. Escape: cancel placement or close a panel/pause.
- F5: quick-save the current named life; creates its first named save when needed.
- F9: open the saved-life picker.

The pause menu opens **Save this life**, with Save as new and confirmed overwrite. The main menu and pause menu open the save picker. **Delete save…** shows the selected name and requires **Delete permanently**; **Keep save** cancels. Deleting a file preserves other saves and the currently open household. Returning to the main menu preserves the current in-memory life; quitting without saving does not.

On Linux, saves normally live at `~/.local/share/godot/app_userdata/JustLife/saves/`, or under the configured XDG data directory. The earlier single-household `justlife_save.json` format remains readable as an Original household entry. Current saves preserve home changes, all household members, queues, paid activity progress, money, time, skills, relationships, stories, age settings/history, school records, adoption history, directed family connections and the venue being visited.

## Development and verification

`python tools/export_linux.py` imports and builds an isolated release snapshot, removing development MCP autoload/plugin configuration from the exported game while retaining the source editor setup. Matching Godot export templates must be installed. The artifact includes its build manifest and font/engine licenses.

`python tests/run_playthrough.py --suite home` or `--suite neighborhood` runs actual rendered gameplay in isolated copies and userdata, then restarts the game to verify persistence. Use `--suite recipes` for cooking selection, serving, leftovers and a paid in-progress restart; `--suite meal_placement` checks crowded dining, furniture removal, supported dishes, idle spacing and washing. The longer `--suite dense_meals --timeout 900` is an exploratory crowded-household scenario with a known late-dinner checkpoint failure, documented in `docs/REVIEWS/iteration_14_dish_placement.md`. `python tests/run_oven_playthrough.py` checks oven loading, closed baking, two paused fresh-process saves, one ingredient charge, the earned first-meal reward and later queued reading. `python tests/run_playthrough.py --suite adoption --adoption-oven --timeout 300` verifies public adoption while preserving a paid oven action, mid-arrival restart, and the new child’s next school day and homework. Add `--adoption-ui-only` for the focused 960×600 candidate/review/cancel flow. These fixtures grant Cooking XP before play and disable autonomy only for the directed arrival checkpoint; the full run enables normal autonomy overnight. Set `TMPDIR` to an existing local directory when temporary storage is limited. Dedicated simulation, actor, build and menu tests are in `tests/`. The directed `res://tests/test_recipe_preparation.gd -- --metrics-only` harness samples animation contacts in a sanitized project with private `XDG_DATA_HOME`; it writes results under `user://recipe_preparation` and does not measure public play. Concise critic reviews are committed in `docs/REVIEWS/`. Screenshots and raw traces are local evidence in `art/screenshots/` or the temporary paths printed by the test runner.

See [the source-tree guide](docs/SOURCE_TREE.md) for the maintained artwork, development tools and local-only artifacts. Godot import settings (`.import`) and resource IDs (`.uid`) remain versioned so fresh checkouts retain the same assets and references.

This is an actively developed game, and the 10/10 target remains open. Current scope does not yet include babies/toddlers, pregnancy/birth, adoption of younger stages, broader parenting systems, death/inheritance, off-household or deceased relatives, step-family labels, stairs/roofs, pets, or the expansive furnishing and occupation catalogs of a commercial life simulation.
