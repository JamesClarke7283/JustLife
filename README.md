# JustLife

An original Godot life simulation with Lifelet creation, a shared household, home design, neighborhood visits and stories that continue across days.

Open `project.godot` in Godot 4.7.2, allow the assets to import, then run the main scene. To create a Linux build, use `python tools/export_linux.py` with matching Godot export templates installed; then open `dist/JustLife/JustLife.x86_64`. Build outputs are local and are not committed. A separately downloaded build runs through `JustLife.x86_64` directly. The Linux build uses Vulkan/Forward+; the verified desktop has an NVIDIA RTX 5090. Other graphics hardware has not yet been qualified.

## Playing

At Normal speed, one real second advances one game minute: a full day lasts 24 minutes. Fast and Very Fast shorten that to eight and three minutes. Pause whenever you want time to plan; walking keeps its physical pace while the daily clock leaves more room for household routines.

Start with **New game**, create up to 8 Lifelets, choose their age, gender (Female/Male), looks, faces, outfits, personality and aspirations, then find a home. The creator offers six hairstyles (crop, bob, curls, ponytail, long, buzz), five tops (casual shirt, jacket, cardigan, tee, hoodie), trousers or shorts, and color swatches for skin, hair, eyes, tops and bottoms; every age stage has the whole set. A wardrobe furnishing lets a Lifelet choose any of the five tops during play, and the portrait follows the change. Child, teen, young adult, adult and elder stages have matching character models; children need a teen or adult housemate. Connections lets a household begin as housemates, siblings, partners, parents and children. Parents must be adults in an older age stage during creation. **My Lifelet → Family tree** shows the household’s generations; choose a portrait card’s name to read connections from that person’s perspective. Click furniture or a person to choose an activity. Each Lifelet walks to their destination before beginning. The household shares money and time, while each person has their own needs, skills, relationships and queue. Select a household chip to take control of another person.

Lifelets approaching activities can step aside to let a housemate pass while keeping their original instructions. When several safe sidesteps work, they compare the stepping distance and the passing route before choosing. This includes a housemate finishing a protected stair exit or following a same-floor walking instruction. A Lifelet heading toward the stairs can also yield to someone returning to a free furnishing, preserving the original upstairs journey and furnishing queue priority through saving and loading. Briefly observing a blocked approach gives housemates an opportunity to yield before choosing another detour. Occupied furnishings have a physical waiting queue, including nearby approaches around corners, preserved across saving and harmless Build edits. Automatic activity changes check the furnishing's availability. Some crowded routes still remain blocked; the [sidestep selection review](docs/REVIEWS/iteration_46_courtesy_ranking.md), [current-floor passage review](docs/REVIEWS/iteration_45_current_floor.md) and [activity-flow review](docs/REVIEWS/iteration_43_activity_flow.md) record the tested recoveries and remaining limits.

**Phone → Adopt a child** lets an adult at home review three school-age Lifelets and choose one or two adult guardians. Confirmation costs §1,000 and requires a free household place; canceling keeps the family and funds unchanged. The child walks home from the street, joins the family tree and becomes controllable immediately. Their first school day is due after the settling-in day. You can pause and save during arrival while other household members keep their existing activities.

Children and teens have a **School** panel. **Go to school** sends them to class on weekdays, 08:00–15:00; arrival after 09:00 counts as late, with departures allowed until noon. Homework prepares the next attended class; lateness and absences affect grades. Adults can **Go to work** on weekdays, 09:00–17:00, with lateness after 10:00 and pay proportional to time at work. Lifelets walk to the street, remain away during the day, and walk home afterward. You can control other household members while they are away, save and resume an absence, or bring someone home early without attendance/pay credit. School and career records offer online classes and home shifts as optional alternatives that share the same daily credit.

**Phone → Household calendar** brings school, work and expected birthdays together. Choose a day in the next two weeks and filter by category. Today's plans show queued, active, away and completed attendance. Birthdays follow the current aging settings; school and work forecasts stop at each Lifelet's next age change and refresh afterward. The calendar pauses play while open and restores your previous speed when closed.

Autonomy plans school, work and homework around urgent needs while preserving queued player instructions. During available school/work preparation, Lifelets prefer a brief fun break before starting a long canvas; Bookworms keep their reading preference. You can still direct any available activity. School records follow a Lifelet into later age stages. **My Lifelet → Celebrate a birthday** advances one stage after a confirmed §30 activity. **Esc → Life settings** changes lifespan or pauses automatic birthdays for the household. Elders currently stay in their final stage.

At a desk, **Do homework together…** lets a child or teen learn with a trusted household adult. Both walk into position, share progress, and gain friendship and learning when they finish. The helper builds Parenting skill. Canceling from either participant ends the shared activity; saving preserves unfinished work. The HUD identifies the partner, and skill bars show progress toward the next level. Short completion messages appear in readable cards beside their speakers; the selected Lifelet has priority when a scene is crowded.

**Cook a fresh meal** opens a cookbook: garden skillet makes four servings for §25, herb garden pasta unlocks at Cooking 2 and makes four for §32, and harvest vegetable bake unlocks at Cooking 4 and makes eight for §52. Each recipe lists its preparation time. Ingredients are charged once when cooking begins; the cook carries the finished dish to a table, and eating restores hunger. Click the served dish to **Call everyone to eat**, take a serving or put away leftovers. Each diner uses a separate plate and available chair. When seats are unavailable, Lifelets walk to reserved standing places that remain clear of other settled activities; building over a dining place reroutes the same partial serving. Fridge leftovers remain selectable until they spoil. Finished meals leave dirty plates that can be carried to a sink and washed. Idle Lifelets may wash dishes after tending their needs and responsibilities. Dishes retain clear support when meals end or furniture is sold; idle Lifelets step aside when crowding another person. Partial meals and their ownership survive saving, and furniture moves preserve the same food. Pasta and bake use original dishes, plates and preparation gestures. The bake now uses an opening oven door and sliding rack: the Lifelet seasons, loads, bakes and retrieves the dish before serving. Paused saves restore the dish and pose during transfer or inside the oven, preserving the one ingredient charge and later instructions.

Maya and Leo walk past your home and leave between walks. Click a passing neighbor to talk; reaching 20 friendship opens **Visit home** in People and their address in **Explore**. Maya lives in a garden cottage with a planted porch; Leo has a wider bungalow with a reading corner and terrace. Their floor plans and furnishings differ. The whole household walks to a shared car, drives across town and arrives outside. Travel advances 15 game minutes; saving becomes available on arrival. Wait for anyone at school or work to return before travelling together. Visits preserve your own home, friendships and resident positions.

From **People**, choose **Invite over** to bring a known neighbor to your home. When they arrive, choose **Welcome in**; your selected Lifelet finishes earlier activities, greets the guest, and lets them inside. The guest card shows how long they will wait or stay. **Say goodbye** sends them home after any conversation already underway. One guest can visit at a time, and building or travelling waits until they leave. Visit progress survives a named save, including an unfinished welcome.

A welcomed guest joins **Call everyone to eat** from a fresh serving dish placed on the ground floor. They collect one serving, use a free chair or standing place, and return to the visit after eating. Sharing a table records company, and their dirty plate can be washed by the household. **Say goodbye** releases the guest's existing portion before they leave; if setdown is blocked, they keep the plate and retry. Saves retain their food, progress and original visit deadline. Guests keep their own homes; hosting parties remains future work. See [guest meals](docs/GUEST_MEALS.md) for the current scope.

Build & buy offers a catalogue of 39 original furnishings across Comfort, Kitchen, Bathroom, Activities and Decor. Beyond the essentials, a household can add an armchair, loveseat and stools, a long-soak bathtub, a home computer, an upright piano, a chess table, a treadmill and yoga mat, a record stereo, a toy chest for children, a wardrobe for changing outfits, a kitchen garden bed, a fireplace, a floor mirror, and wall and side decor. The loveseat seats two Lifelets side by side, and television takes the nearest free sofa, loveseat or armchair so a household can watch together. In a crowded home, leisure autonomy prefers an idle furnishing over a queue at the favourite one and rotates through recent pastimes, and a Lifelet whose bed or fridge is busy for more than forty-five minutes takes the next option rather than standing in line. Each activity furnishing has its own directed and autonomous interaction with a distinct pose: bathing, playing the piano, playing chess, running, stretching, dancing, playing with toys, practicing a speech, warming up by the fire and changing outfits. Skills now cover Cooking, Creativity, Charisma, Logic, Gardening, Parenting, Fitness and Music; older saves begin the newer skills at level 1. Emotions change how fast skills grow: an Inspired Lifelet builds creative and musical skills faster and sells paintings for more, Focused speeds Logic and Cooking, Energized speeds Fitness, Playful and Confident speed Charisma, Happy helps everything a little, and Tense slows learning until it passes; the mood tooltip states the current effect, and an activity's own feeling (reading is Focused, a run is Energized) counts even while a nagging need keeps the mood label at Unsettled. Promotions need both full performance and the career's skill at the current level, and the Career panel names the next requirement. Treadmill runs are for teens and adults, and toys are for children; the rules hold when saved queues reload.

**Explore** also opens Juniper Gardens, The Reading Room and Common Ground Studio. Travel takes 15 in-game minutes and brings the household together. **Stories** presents opportunities and personal memories as the days pass. Adult relationships can become partnerships and commitments once mutual friendship and romance requirements are met; breakups have lasting relationship effects. **Build & buy** at home places, rotates, moves and sells furnishings, builds rooms/walls, opens doorways and changes floors. The catalogue has category tabs and a search box; wall decor (painting, wall clock, shelf) slides along the nearest wall while you place it, faces into the room, and cannot hang in open floor. **Paint wall** in the Structure tab recolours one wall segment at a time from five swatches, priced by length, with undo.

Build & buy supports two storeys, straight stairs with landing rails, and rectangular gable roofs. Choose Ground or Upper before editing. New roof and Edit roof use two corners; R changes the ridge direction, and the pitch and finish controls update the preview. Quotes show the price and any placement conflict before purchase. Undo reverses the latest valid purchase or edit while Build mode stays open.

When bladder is desperate, a plant pot offers **Wee in plant pot (desperate)**. The Lifelet walks there and remains clothed. Leaving the need untreated for too long causes an accident; click the wet patch to **Mop up accident**. Wet patches persist separately on each floor and at visited homes, including after saving. Lifelets finish crossing stairs before a pending accident appears on a supported landing. Build mode protects a wet patch's supporting floor until it is mopped.

- Right-drag or Q/E: orbit. Mouse wheel: zoom. Shift-drag, middle-drag or WASD/arrows: pan.
- Space: pause. 1/2/3: normal/fast/very fast time.
- Ground/Upper or Page Down/Page Up: view a floor in Live mode. Center shows the selected Lifelet’s floor.
- B: Build & buy. R: rotate a furnishing. Escape: cancel placement or close a panel/pause.
- F5: quick-save the current named life; creates its first named save when needed.
- F9: open the saved-life picker.

The pause menu opens **Save this life**, with Save as new and confirmed overwrite. The main menu and pause menu open the save picker. **Delete save…** shows the selected name and requires **Delete permanently**; **Keep save** cancels. Deleting a file preserves other saves and the currently open household. Returning to the main menu preserves the current in-memory life; quitting without saving does not.

Saves and previews live in `~/.justlife/saves/` (Windows: `%USERPROFILE%\.justlife\saves`). The first launch imports older saves from Godot's user data folder, preserving the original files and any existing destination saves. Deleted imports stay deleted after restart. The earlier single-household `justlife_save.json` format remains readable as an Original household entry. Current saves preserve home changes, all household members, queues, paid activity progress, money, time, skills, relationships, stories, age settings/history, school records, adoption history, directed family connections and the venue being visited. `JUSTLIFE_DATA_DIR` can select a different storage root; named saves go in its `saves` subfolder.

## Development and verification

`python tools/export_linux.py` imports and builds an isolated release snapshot, removing development MCP services from the export. It requires matching Godot export templates and includes the build manifest and font/engine licenses.

Use [the test guide](tests/README.md) to choose a focused regression or rendered playthrough. Runners keep saves and Godot user data in private directories. [Release verification](docs/RELEASE_VERIFICATION.md) records packaged checks, hardware, retained failures and the limits of that evidence. Detailed independent findings remain in [the reviews](docs/REVIEWS/).

See [the source-tree guide](docs/SOURCE_TREE.md) for maintained artwork, development tools and local artifacts, and [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md) for asset provenance. Godot import settings (`.import`) and resource IDs (`.uid`) remain versioned so fresh checkouts retain the same references.

## Current scope

The latest full-game independent review is **7.2/10**; the 10/10 target remains open. The game has connected creator, build, household, neighborhood and progression systems. Later focused reviews cover school/work routines, shared meals, oven cooking, adoption, sanitation and two-storey homes; they do not replace a complete independent review.

Character art and acting, household depth and wardrobe choices still need work; the furnishing catalogue was broadened to 39 items in iteration 54, though it remains far smaller than a commercial catalogue. Production adults include the reviewed clothing and Crop hairstyle improvements, [shallower eyes with blended eyelid roots](docs/REVIEWS/iteration_44_adult_eyes.md), [improved chin and mouth support](docs/REVIEWS/iteration_47_adult_lower_face.md), [fuller lip surfaces](docs/REVIEWS/iteration_48_adult_lips.md), and [improved casual drape and shoulder weights](docs/REVIEWS/iteration_52_final_clothing.md). Closed eyelid creases, convex ear pads, a dark lip contour and a slightly angular underside remain visible in close views. Small inner-arm clothing gaps, angular shading on simpler models and mottled seated trouser shading also remain. Walking paths can cross, and crowded households can experience slow cleanup, lateness and prolonged low needs. The game does not yet include babies/toddlers, pregnancy/birth, younger-stage adoption, death/inheritance, off-household or deceased relatives, step-family labels, pets, or the extensive occupation and furnishing catalogues of a commercial life simulation. It does not claim feature parity with The Sims 4.

See [the original quality rubric](docs/QUALITY_RUBRIC.md), [the latest full review](docs/REVIEWS/iteration_10_final.md), and [the reference coverage map](docs/GAME_COVERAGE_MAP.md) for the target and remaining evidence gaps.
